- Introduce Understanding Kit with the explicitly invoked `pop-quiz` skill, based on the SDLC Understanding Pop Quiz v0.3 design.
- Select necessary knowledge from confirmed responsibilities and critical decisions; apply evidence and quiz-suitability gates.
- Run two base questions plus optional adaptive transfer or post-feedback practice, with explicit second-KU continuation and a hard five-question session limit.
- Keep local understanding signals distinct from stable mastery, preserve high-risk unassessed work, and maintain pending unanswered questions within host capabilities.

### Fixed

- Correct redundant checks of already-supported foundations, an additional question that only changed names/values and a mixed wrong-answer pair prematurely attributed to one misconception. Elicit the uncovered task judgment, require a meaningful scenario change and a causal account of both actual selections, and preserve uncertain attribution before feedback.
